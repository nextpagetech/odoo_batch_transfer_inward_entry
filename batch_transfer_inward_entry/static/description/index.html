Odoo 18 Inventory & Purchase

Batch Transfer Inward Entry

Control extra products received on purchase-linked receipts, internal transfers, and batch transfers with a Purchase Manager approval workflow. The addon blocks validation until extra-product exceptions are approved or removed, and approved extras can be added directly to the relevant purchase order.

Overview

Batch Transfer Inward Entry extends Odoo Inventory and Purchase workflows for situations where products arrive that were not part of the original purchase order. It detects those extra stock moves on PO-linked incoming or internal transfers, marks them for review, and prevents transfer validation until the exception is resolved.

Purchase Managers can approve the extra product and create/link the corresponding purchase order line, or reject the extra move before validation.

Key Features

Extra Product Detection

Detects incoming or internal stock moves without a related purchase order line when the transfer is connected to a purchase order.

Purchase Manager Approval

Provides approve and reject actions for extra products, restricted to Purchase Managers.

Validation Protection

Prevents receipt or batch validation while pending or rejected extra products still require action.

Purchase Order Line Creation

On approval, creates or links the relevant purchase order line and keeps the stock move connected to the selected target purchase order.

Add Extra Product Wizard

Lets users add an extra product from the receipt or batch workflow, including quantity and optional purchase pricing details supported by the addon.

Batch Product Summary

Adds a product summary for batch transfers with demand, arrived quantity, approval state, target purchase order information, and detailed operations access.

Lot / Serial Allocation

Supports detailed lot and serial allocation through the batch detail operations workflow, including stock-quant selection for applicable transfers.

Shortfall & Backorder Handling

Tracks quantity shortfalls from removed moves and carries the relevant quantities into backorder processing where applicable.

How It Works

  1. A purchase-linked incoming receipt, internal transfer, or batch contains a product without a related purchase order line.
  2. The addon marks the extra product as pending and prevents validation.
  3. A Purchase Manager reviews the extra product and selects/validates the target purchase order.
  4. Approval creates or links the purchase order line and marks the move approved.
  5. Rejection cancels and removes the extra move, provided no completed quantity prevents rejection.
  6. Once no unresolved extra products remain, the transfer can continue through normal Odoo validation.

Where It Works

Batch Transfers

Product summary, extra-product approval controls, and detailed operations are available directly from the batch workflow.

Receipts / Internal Transfers

Adds extra-product controls, target purchase order selection, approval state, and validation protection.

Purchase Orders

Shows related extra-product activity so Purchase users can review exceptions associated with the order.

Business Benefits

  • Prevents unapproved extra stock from being received silently.
  • Keeps receipt exceptions connected to the relevant purchase order.
  • Provides a clear Purchase Manager approval trail.
  • Reduces manual correction between Inventory and Purchase.
  • Improves batch-level visibility of demanded and actually arrived products.

Requirements

  • Odoo 18.0
  • Inventory / Stock
  • Batch Transfers
  • Purchase
  • Purchase Stock
  • Product Variant Attribute Search dependency

Security

Extra-product approval and rejection actions are restricted to users with Purchase Manager permissions. Stock users can access the operational batch summary and shortfall records according to the module access rules.
